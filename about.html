Our Story & Brand Mission

Building trust through impeccable event delivery, polished hospitality, and convenience across Akure, Ondo State, and Ekiti.

Our Foundation

Organising an Event Should Be Exciting, Not Overwhelming

At Teepearl Event Services, we believe that bringing family, friends, and colleagues together should be a cherished celebration, never a source of anxiety. We are an event service brand based in Akure, Ondo State, created to streamline the entire event planning process.

From professional ushers, drinks chilling, and custom celebration cakes to small chops, event planning, and vendor sourcing, we help clients handle more of their event needs without having to search endlessly for separate contractors.

"Our purpose is simple: to take the stress out of organising events so our clients can focus on enjoying their special moments."

More Than 8 Years of Event Mastery

Our years in the industry have given us practical, hands-on knowledge of what it takes to organize and deliver unforgettable events. Over the years, we have planned, coordinated, and supported weddings, landmark birthdays, political receptions, and corporate galas.

Beyond general planning, we operate our own direct service lines — including high-capacity drinks chilling, live popcorn carts, bride assistance, custom confectionery, and small chops. For services outside what we provide directly, our curated vendor sourcing connects you with premier sound engineers, decorators, and photo/video specialists.
