About Teepearl Event Services

We Make Event Planning Exciting, Not Overwhelming

At Teepearl Event Services, we believe organizing an event should be celebratory, not a chaotic ordeal. Based in Akure, Ondo State and serving Ekiti and neighboring regions, we were founded to streamline your celebration by uniting direct event services and trusted vendor connections under one roof.

What makes Teepearl truly distinctive is our commitment to convenience and uncompromising quality. Whether it’s supplying impeccably dressed ushers to delight your guests, keeping your celebratory drinks frosty cold, crafting showstopping multi-tiered cakes, or coordinating full reception hall logistics, our experienced team ensures you remain a guest at your own party.
